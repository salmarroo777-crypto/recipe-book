# Recipe

## Ingredients
- 2 ripe avocados
- 1/2 onion, finely diced
- 1 tomato, diced
- 1 tablespoon fresh cilantro
- 1 lime, juiced
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
- Step 1: Mash the avocados in a bowl.
- Step 2: Mix in the onion, tomato, and cilantro.
- Step 3: Add lime juice and salt, then stir well and serve!
- Enjoy with chips!
